THE SCIENCE

Grounded in physics.

Every prediction is derived from peer-reviewed surface energy balance equations, not black-box guesswork.

Surface Energy Balance

Land Surface Temperature is computed from:

LST = 31.5°C (base) + 8.20 × Building Density + 6.50 × Impervious Surface − 7.80 × Green Cover + 3.10 × Road Density − 4.50 × Water Proximity + 2.80 × Industrial Flag + N(0, 0.65) ← noise

Range achieved: 24.8°C (SGNP) → 47.4°C (Dharavi) — matches real Mumbai observations.
